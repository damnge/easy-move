Your stay in Poland.
Legal, stress-free.

Umów konsultację

Your case in
good hands

Easy Move was founded out of a genuine commitment to helping people build their lives in Poland. Our founder, Marcin Czaja, spent years working at a Voivodeship Office. This first-hand administrative experience helps us understand how applications are assessed, what documentation is expected, and how to prevent avoidable delays.

We provide comprehensive support — from analysing your situation and preparing documents to accompanying you during your office appointment. Every case receives individual attention.
